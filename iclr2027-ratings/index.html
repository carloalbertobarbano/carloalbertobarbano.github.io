About this data
  • Source. A public snapshot of OpenReview scores published by Paper Copilot (), not a live pull from OpenReview. Anonymous OpenReview API access is behind a bot challenge, so numbers were not cross-checked against the source.
  • Scale. Ratings are 1–4 as recorded in that dataset. The page assumes higher is better (as in earlier ICLR scales); the meaning of each level was not verified.
  • Snapshots.
  • Who is counted.
  • Other trackers. The ICLR 2027 Scores page reported 40,616 active submissions (40,458 rated) on 2026-11-06 13:54 UTC, before the first Paper Copilot ratings snapshot (40,273 active on 2026-11-07 02:15 UTC). The 343 papers in between had been withdrawn; they scored low (mean 1.53 vs 2.07). Active counts keep falling as authors withdraw, so totals depend on when a snapshot was taken.
  • Paper score. The mean of a paper’s reviewer ratings. With 3 reviews it can only be a multiple of ⅓, so ties are common and “top 10%” can’t be split exactly: the chips pick the highest cutoff that still covers at least that share, and the readout shows the real share. Histogram bars cover a third of a point each (the left edge is the cutoff).
  • History. The “What changed” charts use every Paper Copilot snapshot of the file (one every ~8 hours), each verified against its published hash. Snapshot charts (sub-scores, review length, agreement, keywords) use the latest one.
  • Percentiles. Linear interpolation across papers, computed exactly from the per-paper means.
  • Area differences are small (about 0.2 points against a within-area spread of about 0.45) and are descriptive, not a statement about paper quality.
